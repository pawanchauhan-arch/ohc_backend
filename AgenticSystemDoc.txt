# Agentic System for Healthcare Automation – Product Documentation

---

## 1. Introduction

The Agentic System is a proposed enhancement to our healthcare platform, designed to deliver smarter, more proactive, and more efficient patient and staff experiences. By leveraging autonomous software agents, the system aims to automate routine tasks, personalize patient journeys, and ensure timely interventions, all while maintaining a human-centric approach.

---

## 2. Key Use Cases

### A. Proactive Health Assistant
- Monitors patient health data for abnormal results or overdue follow-ups.
- Reaches out to patients with reminders or suggestions (e.g., "You are due for a checkup. Would you like to book an appointment?").

### B. Symptom Triage Agent
- Guides patients through reporting their symptoms in a conversational manner.
- Suggests next steps based on reported symptoms (e.g., consult a doctor, monitor at home).

### C. Medication Adherence Agent
- Reminds patients to take their medications as prescribed.
- Notifies patients about prescription refills or missed doses.

### D. Consultation Scheduler
- Assists patients in booking consultations with available doctors.
- Suggests optimal times based on patient and doctor availability.

### E. Report Summarizer
- Provides patients and doctors with easy-to-understand summaries of health checkup reports and medical history.

### F. Escalation Agent
- Identifies urgent or high-risk cases and ensures they are promptly flagged for human review or intervention.

---

## 3. Potential Workflows

### Workflow 1: Proactive Follow-Up
1. Patient completes a health checkup.
2. The system reviews the results and detects an abnormal value.
3. The agent sends a message to the patient: "We noticed your blood pressure is high. Would you like to schedule a consultation?"
4. Patient responds, and the agent assists with booking or provides further guidance.

### Workflow 2: Symptom-Driven Consultation
1. Patient initiates a chat and reports new symptoms.
2. The agent asks clarifying questions to understand the symptoms.
3. If necessary, the agent suggests booking a consultation and assists with the process.

### Workflow 3: Medication Reminder
1. Patient receives a new prescription with a medication schedule.
2. The agent sends timely reminders to take medications.
3. If a dose is missed, the agent follows up and offers support or information.

### Workflow 4: Escalation of Urgent Cases
1. The system detects a critical lab result or symptom report.
2. The agent immediately flags the case for review by a healthcare professional.
3. The patient is informed that their case is being prioritized.

---

## 4. Benefits

- **Improved Patient Engagement:** Patients receive timely, relevant, and personalized support.
- **Reduced Staff Workload:** Routine queries and tasks are automated, allowing staff to focus on complex cases.
- **Faster Response Times:** Patients get instant answers and support, improving satisfaction and outcomes.
- **Consistent Communication:** Ensures all patients receive the same high-quality information and guidance.
- **Safety and Compliance:** Urgent cases are promptly escalated, and all actions are logged for transparency.
